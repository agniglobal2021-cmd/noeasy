Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Website Owner

This website (noeasy.xyz) is operated by the artist NOEASY. The website serves as a personal portfolio showcasing artworks and providing links to external platforms where the artist's work may be viewed or purchased.

Information Collection

This website does not use cookies and does not require registration. To understand how it is used, the site counts visits anonymously: which pages are opened, how long they stay on screen, the country, the type of device (phone, tablet or computer) and the website a visitor came from. It also counts which artworks are opened in the gallery and in The Halls, entering the cathedral and how long it takes to load, the use of buttons such as Reveal, and links followed to other websites.

The IP address and the browser type are used only for a moment: to find the country, to tell visitors apart within one day and to limit how often requests can be sent. They are never stored, and visitors cannot be recognised from one day to the next. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, the visit is not counted.

Visitors may optionally submit their name, email address, and a message through the contact form available on the website. This information is used only to respond to inquiries and is not stored or shared for marketing or advertising purposes.

Use of Information

Information submitted through the contact form is used solely for communication between the visitor and the website owner. The website owner does not sell, rent, or distribute personal information to third parties.

Newsletter

If you subscribe to the newsletter on the home page, your email address is stored together with the date of subscription and the country detected from your connection at that moment. The address is used only to send news about new works, releases and The Halls. It is never sold or shared.

Every letter contains a link to unsubscribe. You can also ask for your address to be deleted at any time by writing to the address below.

Automation and Content Distribution

The website owner may use internal automation tools to manage and distribute artwork across external platforms. These tools are used exclusively by the website owner to organize artwork, prepare images, and publish content such as pins or posts linking to external art platforms or stores.

These automation processes may involve temporary storage or processing of artwork images and related text information.

Third-Party Services

This website and the owner's workflow may interact with third-party services, including:

  • Formspree (delivers messages sent through the contact form)
  • Pinterest
  • Telegram
  • Google Drive
  • Google Sheets
  • GitHub Pages (website hosting)
  • Railway (visit statistics and the newsletter list)

These services may process data according to their own privacy policies and terms of service. Visitors are encouraged to review the privacy policies of these platforms if they interact with them directly.

External Links

This website contains links to external platforms such as social media profiles and online stores where the artist's work may be displayed or sold. The website owner is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies, and the visit counter works without them. The site may remember a few display preferences in your browser, such as whether sound is on in The Halls or that you have already subscribed.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information submitted through the website. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can contact the website owner at noeasyvision@proton.me.
